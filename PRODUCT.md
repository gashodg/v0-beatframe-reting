# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Live music production teams: bands, promoters, venue managers, and freelance AV technicians renting gear for concerts, festivals, and streaming productions. They know their equipment, work under time pressure, and need to confirm availability, specs, and logistics fast. The checkout is a means to an end — they're thinking about the load-in, not the UI.

## Product Purpose

BeatFrame rents professional audiovisual equipment (cameras, video mixers, wireless transmitters, intercom, professional audio) for live music streaming and realización en vivo. Based in Barcelona. Delivery within 24 hours. Success means a production team has everything they need on stage, on time, with no surprises.

## Brand Personality

Quiet confidence, technical authority, Barcelona edge. No hype, no lifestyle photography overload. The brand knows the gear cold and trusts the work to speak. Spanish-speaking, local, sharp — not placeless SaaS polish.

## Anti-references

Corporate events companies: blue gradient banners, clip-art icons, formal tone, generic "we deliver solutions" copy. Also: consumer electronics retail grids (Amazon/MediaMarkt) with loud badges and price-first hierarchy.

## Design Principles

1. **Gear first, decoration second.** Product images and specs take precedence over visual ornament. The equipment sells itself.
2. **Earned trust, not announced.** Credibility comes from precision: exact specs, clear logistics, honest lead times — not testimonial carousels or badge walls.
3. **Barcelona, not generic.** The brand should feel specific and local. Avoid the placeless polish that could belong to any European tech company.
4. **Ready to shoot.** Every flow moves fast. Operators have load-ins to catch. Friction is a liability.
5. **Dark as default.** Live production happens in dark rooms, under stage lights. The interface belongs in that world.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Reduced motion respected. High contrast between foreground text and dark backgrounds — the site's dark palette requires care to avoid low-contrast muted text.
